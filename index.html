What I can build or fix for you

Hands-on engineering across the whole data lifecycle, from the moment a row changes in your production database to the moment it appears in a report or a model.

Real-time streaming and CDC

Capture every insert, update and delete from your operational systems and make it available for analysis within seconds.

Covers: event streaming, late and out-of-order data, exactly-once delivery, replays

Batch ETL and ELT pipelines

Reliable scheduled pipelines that load incrementally, recover from failure on their own and can be re-run safely.

Covers: orchestration, incremental loads, backfills, dependency management

Migrations

Move warehouses, databases or a whole platform, on-prem to cloud or engine to engine, while the business keeps running.

Covers: parallel runs, reconciliation, cut-over planning, rollback paths

OLAP and data modeling

Analytical stores and models shaped around the questions your teams actually ask, so queries are fast and costs stay predictable.

Covers: warehouse and lakehouse design, dimensional models, query performance, cost

Correctness and data quality

Numbers that match the source system and stay that way, with problems caught before they reach a dashboard.

Covers: validation, deduplication, reconciliation, data contracts, schema evolution

Health metrics and observability

Know whether your data is fresh, complete and on time, and get alerted when it isn't.

Covers: freshness and volume SLAs, data-health KPIs, alerting, incident response

OLTP database reliability

Keep production databases fast and recoverable while analytics reads from them.

Covers: replication, backups, disaster recovery, performance tuning

Data foundations for AI

Fresh, governed data that ML models and LLM applications can rely on.

Covers: feature pipelines, document ingestion for retrieval (RAG), vector search, training data quality

Governance and security

Sensitive data protected and access controlled, without slowing down the people who need it.

Covers: PII encryption and masking, access control, lineage, retention

What every pipeline I ship has

Reconciliation

Freshness and volume metrics

Schema-change handling

A backfill path

Alerts and a runbook

Common questions

Which tools do you work with?

Ideally, the ones you already have. I've built on open-source engines and on cloud services, and the hard problems (correctness, latency, cost and schema change) are the same on all of them. I'll recommend a change only where it clearly pays off.

Do you work with on-prem or cloud setups?

Both. Much of my production work has been self-hosted on Kubernetes, and I've also led an on-prem to cloud migration. I work across AWS, Google Cloud and Azure.

How quickly can you start?

An intro call can usually happen within a few days. Audits can start the following week; larger projects start once we've agreed the proposal.

Can you work alongside our existing team?

Yes, and that's usually the best setup. I join your channels and repositories, pair with your engineers, and leave them able to run what we built.

How do you charge?

Audits and projects are fixed price, agreed before work starts. Ongoing support is a monthly block of hours. I can contract directly or through Upwork.
